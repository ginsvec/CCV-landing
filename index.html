What we clean, and what stays with your team

A written scope based on PICNet guidance and your college's infection control standards. No surprises about who does what.

We clean, every visit

  • Floors in every room, flat mop and HEPA vacuum
  • Washrooms, fully cleaned and restocked
  • High-touch surfaces in reception and waiting room: door handles, switches, counters, card terminals
  • Operatory and exam room floors, walls and cabinet fronts
  • Sterilization room floor
  • Staff kitchen and break room
  • General waste and recycling

Your clinical team handles

  • Dental chairs, exam tables and clinical equipment between patients
  • Instruments, sterilizers and dental water lines
  • Sharps and biomedical waste, through your licensed carrier
  • Blood or body fluid spills during clinic hours
  • Vaccine fridges, specimens and medications

We use only disinfectants authorized by Health Canada, with the product list and safety data sheets in your clinic's binder.

Questions clinics ask

When do you clean?

After you close or before you open, on the schedule that fits your clinic.

Do you clean dental chairs or clinical equipment?

No. Under BC infection control guidance, cleaning between patients is the clinical team's job. We clean the room around it: floors, walls, cabinet fronts and high-touch surfaces outside the clinical zone.

Is the price on this page the real price?

It is the starting price for a typical clinic of that size. After the walkthrough you get a fixed monthly price in writing. It only changes if your clinic's size or schedule changes, or with our yearly review each June.

What happens if our cleaner is sick?

Your named backup covers the visit. You will know who is coming in advance.

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Month to month, with 30 days notice to cancel.

How do we pay?

We invoice on the 1st of each month. Pre-authorized debit is available.

Which areas do you serve?

The City of Vancouver. Ask us about other areas.
